Hello! I'm Christine

Meeting you where you are, and guiding you to where you want to go.

I'm so happy you're here. It is my mission to support you in living a healthier, happier life. Whether that means a stronger, more flexible, vibrant body, a clear, calm, creative mind, or more joy, peace and purpose — I'm here to get you there.

I've been working with thousands of people, in all conditions and ages from children to those in their 90s, for the last twenty-six years. It has been my experience that through the healing and transformation a yoga practice provides, each person comes to a deeper connection to themselves and the world around them — and that has a powerful, positive effect on every aspect of life.

My style draws from the ancient traditions and the modern sages of our time, and weaves together Ayurveda, yogic philosophy, mindfulness, sound healing, classic Hatha, Vinyasa Flow, Iyengar and Kundalini. It's tailored to you, your needs, and your highest vision for yourself.

Credentials and background

Christine stepped into yoga as an actress and a dancer in New York in 1994, and it changed her life. In 2000 she received her first certification in Los Angeles as a 200-hour teacher and has taught full time ever since. She is a Registered Yoga Teacher with Yoga Alliance at the 800-hour-plus level and has taught thousands of students of all ages.

Christine and her husband Gary McCleery founded Liberation Yoga in 2004. The studio was named among the “Top 20 Yoga Studios in the World” by Travel + Leisure, “Best Yoga Studio in LA” by LA Weekly, and one of the “Top Ten Most Beautiful Yoga Studios in the U.S.” by tripping.com.

Her writing has been featured in LA Yoga and Yogi Times; her teaching in the LA Times, LA Weekly, Yogi Times, LA Yoga, Westways, Jane, Great Yoga Teachers and many online magazines. Her first book, The Yoga Healer, was named “Best Yoga Book” of 2017 by Soul and Spirit. It and her second book, The Power of Breath and Hand Yoga, have been reissued and translated into several languages. Her newest, The Little Book of Yoga, was released in 2025.

She has been the yoga and meditation teacher at Marlborough School since 2012, and is the creator of The Spiritual Mental Health Diet, a beloved online program of self-care and self-discovery, since 2017. She is a grief educator through mind-body connection and movement since losing her husband in 2021. She leads healing adventure yoga retreats across the globe and in the U.S. Her most recent passion project is her podcast, Zen Maintenance with Christine Burke.

Christine lives with her daughter Clementine and their pets, sharing the gift of yoga and life in Los Angeles.

Lokah Samastha Sukhino Bhavantu — may all beings everywhere be happy and free.

    The Spiritual Mental Health Diet · since 2017

    40 days to a lighter, brighter you.

    Not a diet — a live-it. Like any good diet we take some things out and add some things in. Over six weeks, drawing on yoga and Ayurveda, we weed out what limits the body and mind and build a toolkit that keeps you connected to your brightest essence — in service of three life goals that matter to you right now.

    • Weekly live group sessions with Christine, from home
    • Practices for mind, body and spirit each week
    • A beautiful workbook for support and reflection
    • Every session recorded — yours to keep
    • An optional in-person finale to practice and gather
